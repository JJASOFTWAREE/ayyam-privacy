Privacy Policy - Ayyam

Updated November 17, 2026. Publisher: JJA Software.

Ayyam does not collect, sell or share your personal information. It needs no account and shows no advertisements.

On your device

Date conversion happens on your device. Your reminders and app preferences are stored on your device only. iOS may include them in your device's own iCloud backup and in transfers to a new phone, according to your device settings; they never reach us. The start date of the free widget trial is kept on your device, and may be kept in your Apple account.

Internet

The app connects to the Internet only to download public updates to its data, such as month-start corrections and the school calendar, and to see whether a newer version of it is on the App Store, over an encrypted connection. It sends no information about you or how you use the app, and without a connection it keeps working with the data it already has.

Notifications

The app asks for notification permission so your reminders arrive on time. Reminders are shown as notifications on your device, and you control them in iOS settings.

Sharing

When you share a date image or copy a date, it goes only to the app you choose.

Purchases

Ayyam Pro purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by the App Store; we never see your payment details. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple account settings.

Deleting your data

Delete any reminder from Your reminders, or remove all app data by uninstalling the app, except the widget trial start date, which stays on your device or in your Apple account and never reaches us. We hold no data about you to delete.
