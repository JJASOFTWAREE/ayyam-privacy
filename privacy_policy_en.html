Privacy Policy - Ayyam

Updated October 27, 2026. Publisher: JJA Software.

Ayyam does not collect, sell or share personal data. There are no accounts, advertisements, analytics or external crash reporting services.

Calendar conversion takes place on your device. Theme, input-calendar and language preferences remain local. Android saves the selected date to restore the screen. Reminder titles, dates, times and annual settings are stored in a private database on your device, excluded from backup and device transfer. They are never sent to the publisher or a server.

The app uses Internet access only to read a public JSON moon-sighting file from GitHub Pages, with jsDelivr as a fallback. Requests use HTTPS. The app sends no selected dates, device identifiers or user-specific parameters. The host may process network information necessary to deliver the public file under its own policy; the app does not use it for tracking.

The last valid public file is stored locally outside backups. Conversion continues when the network is unavailable.

On your first reminder, the app requests notification permission on Android versions that require it and explains access to precise alarms. Boot permission restores saved appointments after restart. Local notifications display appointment information, with a redacted public lock-screen version subject to Android settings. You can change the notification sound or disable reminders in system settings.

When you choose to share, the app creates a temporary PNG containing both dates and your optional caption and passes it to the app you select through Android. Copying writes the date to your device clipboard. These contents are not sent to the publisher.

Delete individual reminders in Your reminders. Delete all reminders, local preferences and cached files by clearing app data in Android settings or uninstalling. No account data is stored on our servers.
