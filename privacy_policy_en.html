Privacy Policy - Ayyam

Updated October 28, 2026. Publisher: JJA Software.

Ayyam does not collect, sell or share your personal information. It needs no account and shows no advertisements.

On your device

Date conversion happens on your device. Your reminders and app preferences are stored on your device only. Android may include them in your device's own encrypted Google backup and in transfers to a new phone, according to your device settings; they never reach us.

Internet

The app connects to the Internet only to update month-start information, over an encrypted connection, and sends no information about you or how you use the app. Without a connection the app keeps working.

Notifications

The app asks for notification and precise-timing permissions so your reminders arrive on time. Reminders are shown as notifications on your device, and you control them in Android settings.

Sharing

When you share a date image or copy a date, it goes only to the app you choose.

Purchases

Ayyam Pro purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your payment details. To run purchases, Google's payment service may collect technical diagnostic information about purchase operations, under Google's privacy policy. You can manage or cancel your subscription in Google Play.

Deleting your data

Delete any reminder from Your reminders, or remove all app data by clearing it in Android settings or uninstalling the app. We hold no data about you to delete.
