Privacy Policy - Ayyam

Updated October 31, 2026. Publisher: JJA Software.

Ayyam does not collect, sell or share your personal information. It needs no account and shows no advertisements.

On your device

Date conversion happens on your device. Your reminders and app preferences are stored on your device only. Android may include them in your device's own encrypted Google backup and in transfers to a new phone, according to your device settings; they never reach us. The start date of the free widget trial is kept on Google servers.

Internet

The app connects to the Internet only to download public updates to its data, such as month-start corrections and the school calendar, over an encrypted connection. It sends no information about you or how you use the app, and without a connection it keeps working with the data it already has.

Notifications

The app asks for notification and precise-timing permissions so your reminders arrive on time. Reminders are shown as notifications on your device, and you control them in Android settings.

Sharing

When you share a date image or copy a date, it goes only to the app you choose.

Purchases

Ayyam Pro purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your payment details. To run purchases, Google's payment service may collect technical diagnostic information about purchase operations, under Google's privacy policy. You can manage or cancel your subscription in Google Play.

Deleting your data

Delete any reminder from Your reminders, or remove all app data by clearing it in Android settings or uninstalling the app, except the widget trial start date, which stays on Google servers and never reaches us. We hold no data about you to delete.
